## Who we are
Mafia Club is a social deduction games club in Jordan. We are the **data controller** of your personal data within the meaning of the Jordanian Personal Data Protection Law No. (24) of 2023.

For anything related to your privacy, contact us at **privacy@masaros.net**

## What we collect
- **Your name, phone number, date of birth and gender** — to create your account, assign seats and know your birthday.
- **Your email address**, if you provide it — optional.
- **Your profile photo**, if you upload one — shown to players at your table and on the venue screen.
- **Your location at the moment of check-in or ordering** — only the latest reading is kept, together with your distance from the venue and the check result. No background tracking, and nothing outside those moments.
- **Your chips balance and its movements and amounts in JOD, your menu orders and their invoices** — financial data that we treat as sensitive data.
- **Your gameplay performance**: your roles, results, rank, attendance record and ratings.
- **Behavioural signals for cheat detection** — explained in their own section below.
- **Your device notification token, device type and app version**.
- **Your conversations with us on WhatsApp**, if you message us.

We do not store your IP address in any log, and we do not record your voice in voice rooms.

## Why we collect it
| Purpose | Data |
|---|---|
| Running the game: seats, roles, results and rankings | Name · Photo · Performance |
| Organising bookings, attendance and orders | Phone · Location · Orders |
| Protecting fair play against cheating and collusion | Behavioural signals |
| Notifying you about things that concern you | Device token |
| Serving you when you message us | WhatsApp conversation |

## Legal basis
Your explicit consent, the performance of the service you requested, and compliance with a legal obligation regarding financial records.

## Who receives your data
- **The game host and club management** — only what their work requires.
- **Players at your table** — your name, photo and seat. This is the essence of the game.
- **Google (notification service)** — your device token and the notification text.
- **Google (automated assistant)** — if you message us on WhatsApp: the text of your conversation, your name and your rank, in order to draft a reply. You can stop it and ask for a human agent at any time.
- **Google Drive** — event photos and videos.
- **Meta (WhatsApp)** — your number and the text of your messages when you message us.
- **Cloudflare** — relaying your connection, and carrying your voice live in remote rooms without recording.

These recipients are **outside the Kingdom**. We transfer the minimum necessary, under a commitment to protect it at a level not lower than what Jordanian law requires. **We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.**

## Event photos
We may photograph events and publish photos on our channels. Photos are shared through restricted links, not public ones. If you appear in a photo and do not want it to remain, tell us and it will be removed.

## How long we keep it
| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Your account data | As long as the account exists |
| After a deletion request | 30 days during which you can change your mind, then erased or anonymised |
| Attendance and location check log | 12 months |
| Cheat-detection signals | 12 months |
| WhatsApp conversations | 24 months from the last message |
| Read notifications | 6 months |
| Financial records | The period required by law, separated from your identity after deletion |

## Your rights
At any time, **free of charge and without any financial or contractual detriment**, you may:
1. **Access** your data and obtain a copy.
2. **Correct** what is wrong or incomplete.
3. **Delete or block** it.
4. **Port** it — a copy in a format usable by another party.
5. **Withdraw consent**. Withdrawing consent for processing that the service cannot work without means the end of the service, and we make that clear before you decide.
6. **Object** to processing, or to unnecessary or discriminatory profiling.
7. **Complain** to the Personal Data Protection Unit at the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship.

All of these are exercised from **Settings → Privacy Centre** in the app, or by contacting us. We respond within thirty days.

## Cheat detection — an explicit clause
- We record when you leave the app during a game and for how long, and whether your role card was open at that time.
- We record the operating system's notice that you took a screenshot. **We do not capture your screen and we do not read the contents of your device.**
- We analyse survival and voting patterns between players to detect collusion. **This is profiling in the legal sense.**
- **The result never applies a penalty automatically** — the decision always belongs to a human, and you may object and request a review.

## Children
An independent account is for people who have reached **eighteen**. For anyone younger, activating the account requires **confirmation by a parent or guardian** with their name and phone number, and the consent is recorded in the guardian's name, not the minor's.

## Security
Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, and access is limited to those who need it for their work.
If a breach affects your data: **we notify you within 24 hours, and we notify the Personal Data Protection Unit within 72 hours.**

## Changes to this policy
A material change requires your consent again, and we show you what changed. A cosmetic change is communicated to you without blocking the app.
