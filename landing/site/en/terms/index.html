Last updated: 29 August 2026

Account

One account per person, with a phone number that belongs to them. You are responsible for your password and for what happens through your account.

Rules of play

  • Follow the host's direction — the host's decision at the table is final.
  • It is forbidden to reveal your role or anyone else's outside the course of the game, to coordinate in advance with another player, or to photograph the screen during play.
  • It is forbidden to impersonate someone else or to use another person's account.
  • Verbal abuse, harassment and discrimination lead to immediate removal.

Violations

Depending on severity: warning · points deduction · removal from the match · account suspension.
You may object to any decision by contacting us, and it is reviewed by a human, not a system.

Bookings and attendance

A booking is not confirmed until we confirm it. Cancellation is available until the event time; after it starts, what was paid is not refunded.
The displayed schedule is an estimate and may shift — we let you know of changes.

Chips

Chips are an in-app balance used in the app's store. They cannot be cashed out or transferred between accounts, and they do not expire.
If you want to delete your account while holding a balance, contact us to settle it before completion.

Content you upload

Your profile photo, ratings and notes remain yours, and you grant us permission to display them inside the app for the purpose they were uploaded for. We remove anything that violates these terms.

Ending the service

You may delete your account at any time from the Privacy Centre, with a thirty-day grace period to change your mind.
We may suspend an account that violates these terms, stating the reason and your right to object.

Limitation of liability

We make reasonable efforts to keep the service available and do not guarantee that it is free from interruption. We are not responsible for what happens between players outside the app.

Governing law
